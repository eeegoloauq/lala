Chat

Bots look like bots

  • Round avatars for everyone. The BOT badge is the marker, in the list, on the tile, in chat and on the profile.
  • The badge comes from the bot- identity, so a bot can't hide it.
  • A bot's status line ("Teardrop · Massive Attack") sits on its tile and its profile. A music bot shows a level meter instead of the green speaking ring.

Commands

  • The profile lists the bot's commands. Clicking one puts it in the message box; it doesn't send.
  • Typing ! in chat suggests commands from bots in the room.
  • Bots publish status and commands through the API. The server checks length and format before others see them.

Listening

  • Off by default. A bot without it gets no audio or video.
  • Before joining: an ear icon on the room and a line on its card. After joining: a toast once, then the ear in the header, list and tile.
  • Every bot in a room sees chat. The chat right only controls sending.
